# User Personas — FitTrack

Personas synthesized from the research findings (see [`../01-research/user-research.md`](../01-research/user-research.md)). Two personas are used because the research surfaced two clearly distinct behavior patterns rather than one.

---

## Persona 1: "Busy Beginner" Maya

```
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│           ( M )              │   Maya Chen, 29
│                              │   Marketing Coordinator
└─────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Goals**
  - Build a basic habit of moving her body 3x/week
  - Feel a sense of progress without becoming a "gym person"
  - Not waste time fiddling with an app after a workout

- **Frustrations**
  - Past apps demanded sets/reps/weight data she doesn't track
  - Long onboarding quizzes before she can even open the app
  - Feels judged by overly "hardcore" fitness app aesthetics

- **Motivations**
  - Visible streaks ("3 days in a row!")
  - Simple weekly summary, not dense charts
  - Quick wins and low commitment

- **Tech comfort:** High with apps in general, low patience for friction
- **Represents needs:** N1, N3, N5

- **Quote:** *"I just want to tell the app 'I worked out' and move on with my day."*

---

## Persona 2: "Structured Improver" Daniel

```
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│           ( D )              │   Daniel Osei, 34
│                              │   Software Engineer, casual lifter
└─────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Goals**
  - Track workouts consistently enough to see real trends over months
  - Know if he's improving (more reps, more consistency, longer streaks)
  - Look back at history to plan future workouts

- **Frustrations**
  - Apps that are *too* simple and lose his history/detail
  - Manually re-entering the same routine every time
  - Gamification (badges, points) that feels like noise

- **Motivations**
  - Clear trend lines and week-over-week comparisons
  - Ability to add detail *when he wants to*, not by default
  - Fast repeat-logging of familiar routines

- **Tech comfort:** Very high, wants efficiency over hand-holding
- **Represents needs:** N2, N4

- **Quote:** *"I don't need a coach. I need my own data to actually be there when I want it."*

---

## Why Two Personas (Not One)

Designing for "everyone" produces an app that satisfies no one. Maya and Daniel pull the design in different directions — minimal friction vs. structured depth — and reconciling that tension is exactly what the user flows and wireframes need to solve (e.g., a default *quick-log* path for Maya, with an optional *add detail* step available but never required, serving Daniel too).
